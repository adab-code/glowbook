# GlowBook — Product Demo Summary

GlowBook solves the scheduling chaos of independent beauty professionals and small studios — lash technicians, hairstylists, nail artists, and estheticians — who today juggle bookings across WhatsApp chats, Instagram DMs, and paper notebooks. Double-bookings slip through, client preferences live only in the owner's memory, and there is no single record of who is coming, for what, and when. GlowBook replaces that patchwork with one studio workspace: an appointment calendar with overlap protection, client profiles with notes and visit history, a service catalog with prices and durations, and a daily dashboard that answers "what does today look like?" at a glance.

The intended users are studio owners and their staff. Owners get full control — they manage the service catalog, enforce role-based permissions, and edit studio settings (name, timezone, currency) that apply instantly across the app. Staff members run the day-to-day: booking and rescheduling appointments, updating statuses, and keeping client notes, while destructive actions (deleting services, clients, or appointments) stay owner-only so a busy front desk can never accidentally erase history. Every studio's data is strictly isolated, and the whole experience works on a phone in the treatment room as well as on a desktop at reception.

The most important flow is the booking loop: sign in, open the dashboard to see today's schedule, book a client into a slot on the appointments page (the app refuses overlaps with a clear conflict message), update the status as the day progresses, and find every visit recorded on the client's profile with its notes. That loop — book, serve, record — is where GlowBook pays for itself: fewer no-shows and double-bookings, client preferences remembered automatically, and a service catalog that keeps pricing consistent. Everything runs on the live deployment with real database-backed data, and the README's "How to test" section walks a first-time evaluator through that exact flow in under five minutes.
